Red Flags to Watch For With Any Cash Buyer

Not every cash home buyer operates the same way. When evaluating any company, watch for these warning signs:

  • No physical address or only a P.O. Box
  • No verifiable BBB listing or business registration
  • Pressure to sign immediately with no time to review
  • Asking for upfront fees before closing
  • No licensed title company involved in the closing
  • Out-of-state phone numbers or call centers with no local knowledge
  • Offers that change significantly between initial quote and contract

Acrux Trust, Inc. operates with none of these practices. All offers are in writing, all contracts use standard Florida forms, and all closings go through a licensed title company.

Common Questions

Is Acrux Trust, Inc. the same as a "we buy houses" company?
Yes, in terms of the core service — Acrux Trust, Inc. purchases homes directly from homeowners for cash, without requiring repairs, agents, or lengthy closing timelines. The difference is that Acrux Trust, Inc. is locally operated by an experienced Miami-Dade real estate professional, not a national franchise or out-of-state investor.
Will Acrux Trust, Inc. lowball my offer?
Cash offers from any direct buyer will typically be below full retail market value — that is the trade-off for speed, certainty, and no fees or repairs. Acrux Trust, Inc. aims to make fair offers that reflect the property's actual condition and the value of a guaranteed, fast close. You are never obligated to accept and can compare offers from multiple buyers before deciding.
Does Acrux Trust, Inc. charge any fees?
No. There are no fees, commissions, or closing costs charged to the seller. The offer you receive is the amount you walk away with at closing, minus any liens or mortgage payoffs that are settled as part of the transaction.
Can I back out after signing a contract with Acrux Trust, Inc.?
Once a seller signs a real estate purchase contract in Florida, they are generally bound by its terms. It is the buyer — not the seller — who has a due diligence or inspection period during which they may cancel. This is why it is important to read the contract carefully and make sure you are comfortable with the terms before signing.
Does Acrux Trust, Inc. buy properties outside Miami-Dade?
Acrux Trust, Inc. is focused primarily on Miami-Dade County but can consider properties throughout Florida. Call (305) 925-2475 to discuss your specific property location.
